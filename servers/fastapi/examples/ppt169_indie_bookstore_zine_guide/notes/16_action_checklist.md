If you’ve read this far, there’s one final step: make a zine yourself. Four steps to get started—one weekend is enough. First, choose a topic you genuinely care about. It doesn’t need to be grand; cats, breakfast, or your commute will do. Second, fold an A4 sheet using the method taught on page seven—it takes thirty seconds. Third, fill these eight pages; handwriting, collage, or photocopies of old photos are all fine. The first one should be rough. Fourth, print ten copies: give five to friends and send five to independent bookstores. The moment you send out your first copy, you become a zine publisher—the threshold isn’t commercial registration, an ISBN, or sales. It’s the second you send it out.
