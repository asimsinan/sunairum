After discussing the methods, let's visit some bookstores. Our first stop is Jimbocho in Tokyo, one of the largest and oldest secondhand-book districts in the world. Between 130 and 180 bookstores are densely clustered around the intersection of Yasukuni-dori and Hakusan-dori, from the long-established Takayama Honten, founded in 1875, to Morioka Shoten, which opened only in 2015—the quirky shop that sells just one book each week. Utrecht in Harajuku also houses one of Japan's largest independent zine collections. In 2025, Time Out named this one of the coolest neighborhoods in the world. For book lovers, it is truly a place of pilgrimage.
