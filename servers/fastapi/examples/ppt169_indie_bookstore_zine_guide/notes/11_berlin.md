The final stop on our tour of independent bookstores in Europe: Bücherbogen in Berlin. Tucked beneath the brick arches of an S-Bahn viaduct, it sits beneath the rumble of passing trains, with shelves devoted to art, design, photography, film, and architecture. It doesn’t sell everything; it sells only these five subjects—and does them to perfection. This is the common model for independent bookstores in Europe: don’t be comprehensive; be absolute.
