After the brief history of the zine, let’s look at its soul: Risograph printing. Risograph is a duplicator product launched in 1986 by Riso Kagaku Corporation, a Japanese company founded in 1946. It was originally created to provide an affordable, eco-friendly printing solution in postwar Japan, but decades later it became a core tool of independent art. Its four defining visual features—limited palettes, misregistered colors, halftone dots, and overprinted ink—are all technical limitations, but artists turned those limitations into a visual language. This page uses misregistration and halftones simulated in SVG.
