So what exactly can you put in the “content”? Here are six common directions: sketches and comics, where ugly handwriting and crooked figures become part of the style; poems, manifestos, and short essays, with the Riot Grrrl Manifesto as a model; illustrated recipes or tutorials, the most widely accessible practical option; family photos paired with personal writing, where autonomy takes precedence over privacy; collages made from torn magazine pages and tape, whose materiality is something a photocopier cannot reproduce; and experimental visuals made purely of solid color blocks or textures — the absence of information is also a kind of information. Choose one or mix them all together; either works.
