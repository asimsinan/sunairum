Turn the lens back to mainland China. This map lists 15 independent bookstores with a zine sensibility, starting with the three most zine-centric destinations: Bananafish in Shanghai—the first art bookstore in mainland China dedicated to independent publishing, with its own Risograph studio; Fake Magazine in Ningbo—one of the earliest independent photography publishers in China; and Wild Dog in Zhengzhou—which carries more than 200 independent magazines from China and abroad. Below that are Yipian Old Shop in Zhoushan, Combo in Wuhan, and Protopaper in Xi’an—all hybrid spaces that also engage in independent publishing. Further down are bookstores focused mainly on international magazines or general literary selections. The list is not exhaustive, but follow this map around and the Chinese zine scene begins to take on a complete shape.
