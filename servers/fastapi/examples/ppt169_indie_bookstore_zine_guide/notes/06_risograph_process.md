The Risograph workflow actually takes only five steps. First, receive a digital image; second, burn the image into tiny pixel holes on a fibrous master; third, wrap the master around a colored ink drum, with each drum containing only one color; fourth, roll the drum to press the ink onto paper; fifth, switch drums and print again—multicolor prints are completed by passing the paper through multiple times. Each pass produces a one- to three-pixel shift. This is registration misalignment—in other printing processes, it is a defect; on the Risograph, it is a signature.
