Many people confuse concealing one’s weaknesses with passive withdrawal from the world. They may look similar on the surface, but are opposites at heart. Passive withdrawal comes from fearing defeat, so one hides; the more one hides, the more useless one becomes. Silence is an excuse, and all that remains inside is a sense of grievance. Concealing one’s weaknesses comes from enduring in order to win; the more one conceals, the stronger one becomes. Silence is preparation, while plans are constantly taking shape inside. Truly strategic people may appear motionless on the surface, but beneath it, they never stop growing—broadening their knowledge, honing their abilities, filtering their relationships, building resources, and learning the rules.
