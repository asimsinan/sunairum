The mistake young people make most often is mistaking "being seen" for "being respected." As soon as they achieve a little, they want everyone to know; as soon as they gain some resources, they cannot resist showing them off; as soon as they see through a little of human nature, they rush to expose it. They think they are clear-eyed, but in fact they have merely placed themselves in the open—people in the open have no cover, so every test, jealous impulse, attempt to exploit, and act of sabotage comes at them first.
