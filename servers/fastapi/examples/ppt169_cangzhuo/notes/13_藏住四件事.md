Specifically, concealing one's strengths comes down to four principles in practice. First, conceal your emotions so you won't be provoked by a few words; second, conceal your ambitions so others won't become wary of you prematurely; third, conceal your abilities so others won't exploit them too soon; fourth, conceal your judgment so you won't be forced to take sides before the situation becomes clear. Bow your head when necessary to keep the blade from striking bone; remain silent when necessary to save your words for when they carry more weight; step back when necessary to leave ample room for your next move.
