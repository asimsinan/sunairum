At a late-night barbecue stall, there are often two kinds of people. One has just been promoted and immediately starts talking about the resources at their disposal, who they know, and how many cards they can play. The others flatter them to their face while calculating who they can borrow from, pressure, or use. The other kind says little, asks only a few key questions, then simply smiles and raises a glass. By the time everyone leaves, the quietest person has seen through nearly everyone’s hand. Most games in life are not lost because people lack ability, but because they reveal their abilities too soon.
