Concealing one’s brilliance does not mean playing dumb, admitting defeat, or withdrawing from the world. It means three specific things: first, restraining your edge—when you lack the strength to protect yourself, your edge becomes a knife handed to others; second, suppressing your desires—being eager for recognition means handing yourself over to others’ judgment; third, moving yourself out of the center of attention—taking one step back from center stage makes it harder for others to set traps for you in advance.
