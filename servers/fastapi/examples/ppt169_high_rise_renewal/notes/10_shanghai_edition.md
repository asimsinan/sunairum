Next is Shanghai. The building at 201 Nanjing East Road was built in 1988. Originally the East China Electric Power Building, it stands 126 meters tall and was the first high-rise on Nanjing Road after the founding of the People’s Republic of China. Its story spans 35 years: completed in 1988; renovated and expanded with No. 181 next door into The Shanghai EDITION in 2018; and upgraded for energy efficiency in 2023, becoming the first hotel in China’s urban-renewal sector to achieve carbon neutrality. The renovation introduced a complete suite of green systems, including high-performance windows, air-source heat-pump heat recovery, energy-efficient elevators, and water-saving fixtures.
