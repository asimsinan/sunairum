So where does the true weight of this regeneration lie? Three numbers. Construction waste was reduced by 80%, carbon emissions were 40% lower than with demolition and reconstruction, and energy consumption fell by more than 30%. It was precisely because of these three figures that, in October 2025, it became a finalist for the “Earthshot Prize,” launched by Britain’s Prince William, making it the first building ever nominated for the award. This tells us that old supertall buildings are not burdens: through scientifically guided renovation, they can be reborn and even become new urban landmarks.
