The third case takes us to New York. This is One Wall Street, a 1931 Art Deco skyscraper in Manhattan’s Financial District, designed by renowned architect Ralph Walker and rising 50 stories. It has now been converted from an office building into 566 apartments, ranging from studios to four-bedroom units. As we see on this tour, the vast majority of successfully converted high-rises were formerly office or commercial buildings. The reason is practical: these buildings generally have stronger structures than residential properties, offering greater flexibility and making it easier to introduce new uses.
