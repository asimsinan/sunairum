The presentation is divided into four parts. The first is a wake-up call, beginning with the Hong Kong fire and examining the systemic risks behind the aging of high-rise buildings. The second looks at a model: the Sydney skyscraper hailed as a “regeneration revolution.” The third is a tour of adaptive-reuse projects in Shenzhen, Shanghai, and New York. The fourth returns to China to discuss the truly tough challenge of aging high-rise residential buildings.
