On November 26, 2025, a massive fire broke out at Wang Fuk Court in Tai Po, Hong Kong. All seven buildings affected were over 30 stories tall, nearly 100 meters high, and already 42 years old. The fire not only shook Hong Kong; it also served as a wake-up call for mainland cities undergoing urban renewal. Data from CNR are even more alarming: in just the first eight months of 2025, there were 36,000 high-rise building fires nationwide, already exceeding the total for all of 2023. Ground-based fire-rescue equipment can typically reach only 50 to 80 meters; beyond that, responders must rely on the building’s own fire-safety systems.
