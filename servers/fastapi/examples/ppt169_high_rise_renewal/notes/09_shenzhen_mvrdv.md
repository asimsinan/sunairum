Bring our gaze back home. This is the Shenzhen Women and Children’s Building, originally built in 1994 and redesigned under the direction of MVRDV. The most interesting design feature is a “colorful grid overcoat”: the facade gains nearly one meter of depth, providing shade while incorporating openable panels on the inside to promote natural ventilation and significantly reduce reliance on air conditioning. The renovated building combines a hotel, restaurants, a children’s discovery center, a picture-book museum, an infant and toddler care center, and a women’s and children’s theater, making it a landmark example of adaptive reuse in urban renewal.
