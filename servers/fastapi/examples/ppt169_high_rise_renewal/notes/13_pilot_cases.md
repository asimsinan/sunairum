There have already been several noteworthy experiments in China. The first is “Office Ring Orange” in Tiantongyuan, Beijing, which converted high-rise residential buildings into offices in an effort to address Tiantongyuan’s long-standing separation between jobs and housing. During the renovation, parts of the original beams and columns were reinforced, and the residential layouts were adapted into office layouts to meet the needs of small and medium-sized enterprises. The second is Zhejiang Gongxin Village in Hangzhou, the first old residential community in China to truly achieve “demolition and reconstruction on the original site.” Thirteen original buildings were demolished and seven new 11-story mid-rise buildings were constructed. The total reconstruction cost was approximately RMB 530 million, of which residents raised RMB 470 million themselves, contributing between RMB 100,000 and RMB 1 million per household.
