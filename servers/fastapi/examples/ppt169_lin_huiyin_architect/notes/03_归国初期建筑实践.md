[Transition] Some question Lin Huiyin's legacy as an architect, saying she "left behind few works." But note that this was not due to a lack of talent—it was a consequence of her times.

In 1929, shortly after returning to China, she and Liang Sicheng completed their first project—a tombstone designed for her father, Liang Qichao. That same year, she contributed to the design of Jilin University’s Stone Building at the Liang-Chen-Tong-Cai Architectural Office. Most astonishing of all was the Jihai Railway Terminal: viewed from above, this Gothic building resembles a crouching lion, with its “tail” cleverly designed as a clock tower, symbolizing “China awakening like a lion.”
