[Transition] Beyond architecture, Lin Huiyin’s academic contributions were equally—if not even more—remarkable and far-reaching.

In 1932, at just 28, Lin Huiyin published “Several Characteristics of Chinese Architecture.” It was the first architectural theory paper published by a Chinese scholar. Before this, Western scholars viewed Chinese architecture as merely “a lone leaf” on the main branch of world architecture. Lin Huiyin rigorously challenged this prejudice, defining for the first time the fundamental characteristics of China’s timber-frame structure in theoretical terms.

[Pause]

Her daughter, Liang Zaibing, once recalled: “She was the central figure.” This was by no means mere praise.
