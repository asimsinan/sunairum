[Transition] After the war, Lin Huiyin devoted the last of her strength to the nation’s most important buildings.

In 1950, she designed the main layout of the Babaoshan Revolutionary Cemetery. In 1952, she was appointed a member of the Architectural Committee for the Monument to the People’s Heroes. She proposed a creative revision: changing the steps from one tier to two, allowing the monument to better match the spatial scale of Tiananmen Square. Using Tang dynasty style as her model, she also personally designed the full set of decorative patterns and wreath reliefs for the monument’s base.

[Pause]

Later, she was buried at Babaoshan, which she had designed herself. Her tombstone bears only seven characters: Architect Lin Huiyin's Tomb.
