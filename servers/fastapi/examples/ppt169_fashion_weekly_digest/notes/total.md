# 02_Hermès Home

[Transition] First, let's begin with the highlight of Milan Design Week.

Hermès Home artistic directors Charlotte Macaux Perelman and Alexis Fabry have created a wandering journey blending architecture, craftsmanship, and imagination with thirty rectangular columns adorned with geometric wooden motifs. The hammered metal reveals suppleness, while the leather inlay creates rhythm—the dialogue between materials is the central thread of this collection. [Pause]

Key points: ① New home-furnishing launches at Milan Design Week ② Thirty columns create an immersive setting ③ A dialogue between materials and the narrative of objects
Duration: 1 minute

---

# 03_Chanel Beach

[Transition] From home aesthetics to holiday moments.

The CHANEL COCO BEACH 2026 Collection pop-up boutique has opened in Shanghai. Stepping inside feels like entering a holiday villa overlooking a garden. Pastoral landscapes and black sand beaches, painted skies and coral accents—this is Matthieu Blazy’s first COCO BEACH collection. [Pause]

Key points: ① Shanghai pop-up boutique concept ② Matthieu Blazy’s first COCO BEACH ③ Dual inspiration from the countryside and the beach
Duration: 1 minute

# 04_Dior: Two Sides

[Transition] Dior has two major highlights debuting this week.

One side is the red carpet—Sabrina Carpenter arrives at Coachella in a gown masterfully crafted by Jonathan Anderson. The other is timekeeping—the Chiffre Rouge watch collection returns refreshed, with three limited-edition pieces coming soon. The striking red seconds hand pays tribute to the passionate, intense color of life in Monsieur Dior’s imagination.

Key points: ① A dual narrative of fashion and timepieces ② Celebrity appearances at Coachella ③ Three limited-edition watches launching at the end of April
Duration: 1 minute

# 05_Louis Vuitton Home

[Transition] Let's continue our stay at Milan Design Week.

Louis Vuitton presents the Objets Nomades travel and home collection, paying tribute to Art Deco and contemporary design. The Pierre Legrain Hommage collection reimagines classics by the master of Art Deco, while new works by contemporary designers including Campana Studio and Raw Edges are equally dazzling. The Collar lounge chair, the Aqua dining table—each brings together exceptional craftsmanship and creative vision. [Pause]

Key points: ① New pieces from the Travel Home collection ② A tribute to Art Deco ③ Collaboration among multiple contemporary designers
Duration: 1 minute

---

# 06_Gucci Memories

[Transition] From the contemporary innovations of Design Week to a brand’s century-old memories.

The Gucci Memoria immersive exhibition opened in Milan, curated by Demna, tracing the brand’s 105-year evolution. Twelve tapestries form the heart of the exhibition, each distilling key milestones in the brand’s development and the design visions of its successive creative directors. The Flora flower-themed garden installation adds an interactive dimension to the exhibition. [Pause]

Key points: ① An immersive exhibition curated by Demna ② A 105-year brand chronicle ③ Flora floral installation and exclusive pre-sale
Duration: 1 minute

---

# 07_The Spirit of Jewelry

[Transition] From exhibition spaces to the world of luxury goods.

Two brands, two interpretations of elegance. Vhernier has officially entered mainland China, opening stores in Beijing and Shanghai simultaneously. With Milanese purity of design and sculptural forms, it showcases the unique allure of Italian high jewelry. CELINE's Été summer campaign, meanwhile, pays tribute to French lifestyle aesthetics through the carefree spirit of a seaside holiday.

Key points: ① Vhernier’s first store in China ② CELINE’s French summer style ③ A dialogue between two forms of elegance
Duration: 1 minute

---

# 08_Artistic Expression

[Transition] Art is fashion's eternal companion.

BV and McQueen engage with art in radically different ways. Bottega Veneta joins forces with photographer Peter Fraser, using 27 images of Venice to interpret woven aesthetics; McQueen brings its “Beneath the Surface” exhibition to Fotografiska Shanghai, while the Manta bag recreates the 2010 classic. Inspired by the manta ray, it explores the rugged reality beneath a polished surface.

Key points: ① BV’s art photography book ② McQueen’s immersive exhibition in Shanghai ③ Two radically different artistic expressions
Duration: 1 minute

---

# 09_A New Chapter of Heritage

[Transition] The meaning of heritage lies in being constantly rewritten.

Max Mara has announced that its 2027 Resort collection will make its global debut at Shanghai's Long Museum, alongside an exhibition of archival treasures celebrating its 75th anniversary. Meanwhile, Chloé has teamed up with Poltronova to reissue the 1970 Tomato chair, an iconic piece of Italy's radical design movement, now reborn in natural leather.

Key points: ① Max Mara’s global debut in Shanghai ② 75th-anniversary archive exhibition ③ Chloé Tomato chair limited reissue
Duration: 1 minute

---

# 10_Structural Reshaping

[Transition] Structure is the skeleton of design.

TAD FAB uses zipper language to reconstruct bag structures, with its wraparound-zipper Hobo balancing capacity and clean lines. Apede Mod, meanwhile, marks an evolution in its design language on its 10th anniversary with its Origami collection—the folds metaphorically express women’s power to shape themselves, while restrained, sharp creases reveal a refined, elevated aesthetic.

Key points: ① TAD FAB’s zipper design language ② Apede Mod’s 10th-anniversary origami collection ③ Structure as aesthetics
Duration: 1 minute

---

# 11_A New Sensory Chapter

[Transition] From sight to scent, the sensory experience is upgraded across the board.

BOSS launches King's Heart, its first foray into a fresh woody-leather fragrance—its dual-polar scent releases woody vitality and leather elegance in tandem. Inspired by tea culture, fresh teams up with CORTIS to launch “Ferment Your Way Wild,” blending Eastern philosophy with youthful energy.

Key points: ① BOSS’s first fresh woody-leather scent ② Inspiration from fresh tea-drink culture ③ A youthful expression of beauty and fragrance
Duration: 1 minute

# 12_A New Cross-Disciplinary Trend

[Transition] Cross-disciplinary collaboration is the spark of creative collision.

JORYA teamed up with YVMIN to create the "Princess Diaries" pop-up in Chengdu, with Zhao Lusi making an appearance to lend her support. The concept of layered, delicate tulle for heiresses redefines contemporary princess style. In Milan, ARKET and artist Laila Gohar transformed a carousel into a giant fruit-and-vegetable sculptural installation, with 27 collaborative pieces set to launch in China.

Key points: ① JORYA Princess Diaries pop-up ② ARKET Milan art installation ③ Diverse expressions through cross-industry collaborations
Duration: 1 minute
