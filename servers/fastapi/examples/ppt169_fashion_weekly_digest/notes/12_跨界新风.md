[Transition] Cross-disciplinary collaboration is the spark of creative collision.

JORYA teamed up with YVMIN to create the "Princess Diaries" pop-up in Chengdu, with Zhao Lusi making an appearance to lend her support. The concept of layered, delicate tulle for heiresses redefines contemporary princess style. In Milan, ARKET and artist Laila Gohar transformed a carousel into a giant fruit-and-vegetable sculptural installation, with 27 collaborative pieces set to launch in China.
