Today, we revisit Lin Huiyin—not through legend or gossip, but by starting with a more accurate identity: architect.

[Pause]

Seventy years after her death, she is still often surrounded by the halo of literature, emotional narratives, and the imagined figure of the “talented woman of Republican-era China.” But if we place her back in the history of modern Chinese architecture, her position is actually quite clear: she not only participated in architectural practice, but also in theoretical development and the founding of the discipline.

This short talk isn't meant to “add another chapter to the story,” but to correct a title.

Key points: ① The theme is not legend, but correcting identity ② Clearly establish three main lines: work, theory, and action ③ Set a professional tone for the entire presentation
