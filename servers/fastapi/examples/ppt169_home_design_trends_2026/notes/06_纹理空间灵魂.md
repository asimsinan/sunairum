[Transition] The third element is texture—often considered the easiest to overlook, yet also the most soulful.

Texture is like the “fingerprint” of a space, giving each one a unique identity. We can understand it from two dimensions: tactile texture and visual texture. Tactile texture is something you can perceive directly—the soft warmth of a wool carpet beneath your bare feet, or the uneven surface of a rough ceramic vase beneath your fingertips. [Pause] Visual texture is subtler—when you look at wallpaper with a delicate pattern, your eyes can “feel” its texture even without touching it. Together, these two forms of texture breathe soul into a space.

Key points: ① Texture = the fingerprint of a space ② Direct perception of tactile texture ③ Imagination and sensation evoked by visual texture
