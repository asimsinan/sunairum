[Transition] So let's begin with the first element—color.

Color is a space’s first impression—it’s like an “emotional magician.” When you enter a room, the first thing you sense is not the brand of the furniture or the cost of the materials, but the color atmosphere of the space as a whole. Warm tones make you feel gently enveloped, while cool tones make the same area seem more expansive. [Pause] Different colors also trigger different psychological responses—gray-green inspires calm and focus, warm coffee tones create the comfort of being wrapped in sunlight, while small touches of accent color can give a space a distinctive personality.
