Let me close with Müller-Brockmann’s most frequently quoted line: the grid is not a constraint. It is a grammar of freedom. If you take away just one thing today, I hope it is this: when a designer complains that the grid limits creativity, what they are really complaining about is that they have not yet learned how to use it; when a designer truly masters the grid, what they gain is not confinement, but a universal framework capable of supporting almost any content, style, or medium. Thank you, everyone.
