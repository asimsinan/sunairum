Seventh stop: Abu Dhabi. This is the final masterpiece left to the world by architectural maverick Frank Gehry. Gehry passed away in December 2025, while the Guggenheim Abu Dhabi, to which he devoted nearly two decades, is expected to open in 2026. Irregular curves, fluid folds, and highly dramatic geometric forms define the building, along with a shading system tailored to the desert’s extreme climate. Its metal façade shifts in texture as the sunlight moves. As the deconstructivist master’s swan song, this building offers Gehry’s final interpretation of his belief that architecture has no boundaries and creativity no limits.
