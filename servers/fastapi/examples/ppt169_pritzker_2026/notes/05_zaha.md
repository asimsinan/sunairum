Third stop: the Tamsui River estuary in Taipei. Opened to traffic on May 12, 2026, the Tamsui River Bridge is one of the final projects completed by the firm Zaha Hadid was part of during her lifetime. Stretching 920 meters, it is the longest-span single-tower asymmetric cable-stayed bridge of its kind in the world. Most moving of all, after numerous rounds of design challenges, the project ultimately realized Zaha’s nearly idealized vision with close to 100% fidelity. Though the master is gone, her architectural spirit of breaking boundaries and refusing limits continues to thrive in this bridge.
