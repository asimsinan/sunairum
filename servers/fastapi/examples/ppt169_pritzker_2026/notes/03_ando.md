First stop: Suzhou. In January 2026, the H+ Art Museum, designed by Tadao Ando, officially opened on the banks of the Shangtang River in Gusu District. Ando said that, as an architect from outside the region, his greatest concern was how to draw the essence of classical gardens from Suzhou’s cultural memory. Layered gardens and circular circulation are the museum’s core concepts—creating depth within a limited space and conveying the passage of time through water and light. From the cool severity of concrete to the tenderness of light, and from geometric order to the freedom of gardens, Ando has completed a profound dialogue between Eastern aesthetics and Western modernism on the banks of the Shangtang River in the ancient city.
