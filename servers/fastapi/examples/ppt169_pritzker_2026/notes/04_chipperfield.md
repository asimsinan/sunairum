Second stop: Milan. This is the only new permanent venue built for the 2026 Milan–Cortina Winter Olympics—the San Giulia Ice Hockey Arena, designed by David Chipperfield. The elliptical venue, which seats 16,000 spectators, echoes the ancient Roman amphitheaters of a city with 2,000 years of history. Three metal rings, a glass belt, and LED light strips transform the façade into a dynamic screen at the scale of the city. Chipperfield never relies on extravagant forms to attract attention; instead, with utmost restraint and precision, he explores architecture’s relationship with history and urban public life.
