After visiting these eight buildings, we have also completed our journey through the 2026 season of new works by Pritzker laureates. Some spent twenty years refining a single venue, using extraordinary patience to resist the impatience of the times; some remained committed to local culture, allowing architecture to take root in the land and warm the heart; others broke boundaries and overturned conventions, using creativity to expand architecture’s infinite possibilities. This feast has taught us that all timeless classics stem from day after day of perseverance, from the deepest reverence and reflection on space, nature, and humanity.
