# 01_cover

Welcome to the SUGAR RUSH 2026 Summer Music Festival Annual Guide. This is more than a schedule—it’s a love letter to summer. Over the next seven days, we’ll bring you all the sweetness in the world at Coconut Island Prairie, all at once.

---

# 02_what

So what exactly is SUGAR RUSH? As the whole world gets hotter, we decided to make it sweeter. It's a seven-day musical celebration, and more than that, an attitude—distilling the best part of summer into a cup of steaming syrup.

---

# 03_numbers

Here are a few key figures. Seven consecutive days of celebration, five themed stages, 80 acts taking turns, and an expected audience of 50,000. Most importantly, music runs nonstop for 12 full hours every day, from 4 p.m. to 4 a.m. the next day. All five stages operate simultaneously, and even the longest walk between venues is only 600 meters.

---

# 04_principles

Three simple ideas support all of this. First, FREE. We don’t create a hierarchy of disdain; every style is a good style. Rock fans can love hip-hop, and everyone is free to switch camps. Second, FUN. Have as much fun as possible and leave with the sweetest memories. Third, FOREVER YOUNG. Whether you’re eighteen or eighty, there’s no age on the dance floor—your heartbeat is the only admission ticket.

---

# 05_who

Next, let’s meet the stars of this celebration. Eighty acts, five style camps, but tonight, all you need to do is find the one face you won’t forget.

---

# 06_headliners

Four headlining acts from completely different worlds. Pink-haired CHERRY BOMB, a rock vocalist known for Pink Static. NEON CLOUD in gold sunglasses, an electronic DJ and resident at Berlin’s Berghain. MINT FOLK in a mint-green shirt, a folk guitarist with 4.8 million monthly Spotify listeners. YELLOW HUSTLE in a bright-yellow hoodie, a hip-hop rapper and XXL Freshman 2025. Four voices, four personalities — every one indispensable.

---

# 07_flavors

Sort these 80 acts by style, and they fall neatly into four camps. Twenty-five rock acts, headlining July 19 and playing all night. Thirty electronic acts, taking over for 12 hours overnight on July 20. Fifteen folk acts, in a twilight showcase on July 21. Ten hip-hop acts, blowing up the midnight stage on July 22. There is also a hidden camp called CROSSOVER, which we’ll reveal later.

---

# 08_where

Now, let’s talk about where and when. Coconut Island Prairie—a city built for summer, with 120 hectares of open space and five major stages surrounding a central lawn. It’s 30 kilometers by car from the nearest airport, and the high-speed rail station shuttle takes just 15 minutes. From July 18 to 24, seven full days, sweet right down to the final minute.

---

# 09_stages

Each of the five stages has its own personality. The central SUGAR DOME is the main stage, with a capacity of 30,000, hosting the opening and closing ceremonies and the crossover night. The pink CHERRY STAGE is for rock, the blue NEON GRID for electronic music, the green MINT GROVE for folk’s quiet whispers, and the yellow YELLOW BLOCK for hip-hop’s street energy. No two stages are more than 600 meters apart on foot, and a shuttle arrives on demand within 30 seconds.

---

# 10_schedule

See the full seven-day schedule at a glance. Day 1, July 18: opening night, ignited by CHERRY BOMB. Day 2: Rock Day. Day 3: an all-night electronic music marathon lasting 12 hours. Day 4: a folk afternoon. Day 5: a hip-hop midnight. Day 6, July 23: crossover night, with four headliners sharing the stage. Day 7, July 24: a grand closing celebration, with fireworks filling the sky above SUGAR DOME. Doors open at 4 p.m. every day, performances begin at 5 p.m., and the event ends at 4 a.m. the following morning.

---

# 11_vibes

But SUGAR RUSH has never been just about music. Over seven days, we’ve prepared at least ten ways to make you feel sweet. They happen beyond the stage—in every time you look up, and every time you turn a corner.

---

# 12_beyond

Two main attractions keep you engaged. At MARKET, more than fifty independent stalls by designers from twelve cities offer limited-edition goods, handmade crafts, and local snacks. At ART INSTALL, ten giant geometric inflatable sculptures stand on the lawn; scan the code to trigger AR interactions, and after dark the entire site transforms into a neon art park. Both the market and installations are included with the all-access pass, with no extra charge.
