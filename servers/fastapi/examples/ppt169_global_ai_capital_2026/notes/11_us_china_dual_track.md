U.S.–China AI capital has become structurally differentiated. The U.S. relies on mega-sized private placements, with individual rounds ranging from 30 billion to 122 billion; China relies on smaller private placements plus IPOs, with individual rounds ranging from 2 billion to 4 billion. U.S. IPOs have been delayed, while China brought Zhipu and MiniMax to the Hong Kong stock market as early as January. The U.S. is market-driven, while China is led by major funds. Peak valuations reached 852 billion in the U.S. and 56.1 billion in China. The two structures have largely solidified in 2026, and the divergence in their paths is no longer reversible.
