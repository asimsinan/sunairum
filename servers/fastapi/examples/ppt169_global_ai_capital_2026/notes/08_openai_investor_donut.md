Where did the money come from for OpenAI's largest-ever $122B financing round? Amazon committed up to $50B, or 41%. Nvidia contributed $30B, or 25%. SoftBank contributed $30B, or 25%. Microsoft, a16z, and others make up the remaining approximately $12B, or 9%. Note a structural observation: these three largest investors together account for 91% and are also OpenAI's largest customers—the investors and the buyers are the same group.
