The lens turns to China. DeepSeek has secured the first lead investment from the national Big Fund, with a valuation of 50 billion, making it the true leader of China’s top AI tier. Zhipu’s Hong Kong-listed market cap is 56.1 billion, and MiniMax’s is 37.2 billion—both reflecting secondary-market pricing after their Hong Kong listings in January. Moonshot has a private-market valuation of 20 billion, with a 2 billion single-round investment—the largest in the history of China’s foundation models. StepFun is negotiating at around 8 billion. One data caveat: Hong Kong-listed market caps and private-market valuations cannot be compared directly; only their order of magnitude can be assessed.
