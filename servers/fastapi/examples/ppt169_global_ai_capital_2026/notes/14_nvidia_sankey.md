Map Nvidia's circular investment as a flow of funds. On the left, Nvidia invests $40 billion in equity. In the middle, $30 billion goes to OpenAI, approximately $3 billion to xAI, approximately $2 billion to Anthropic, approximately $1 billion to Mistral, and $4 billion to CoreWeave and others. On the right, these AI companies then funnel most of the money back to Nvidia as multiyear commitments to purchase chips—more than $100 billion is expected to flow back. A Wedbush analyst's comment is exceptionally precise: this is exactly the circular-investment theme the market is most concerned about.
