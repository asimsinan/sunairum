A scissors chart. The red line shows hyperscaler capital expenditure, rising from $20 billion in 2020 to $725 billion in 2026—almost a vertical takeoff. The green line shows enterprise AI revenue, growing from several billion dollars in 2020 to approximately $100 billion in 2026, with a much gentler slope. The shaded area between the two lines is the ROI chasm. Capitalism is patient, but its patience is limited.
